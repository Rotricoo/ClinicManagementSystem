Every medical staff member has a name, staff ID, and hours worked today.

There are two subclasses:

Nurse has a specific attribute to track how many patients they attended today.
Nurse also has a method to record each newly attended patient.

Doctor has a specific attribute for the shift operational budget.
Doctor also has a method that checks whether a given amount is still within the budget.

Every patient has a name, contact details, and number of visits.

VIPPatient adds a priority care tier and overrides the priority calculation so VIP patients receive higher priority than regular patients.
